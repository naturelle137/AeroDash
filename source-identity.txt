    * fix(mb): use total-moment difference F(q)-F(u) for lookup fuel tanks
    
    Lookup tables hold the total tank moment at each load while unusable fuel
    stays in BEM, so the usable contribution is F(q)-F(u), not (q-u)*F(q)/q.
    Strict geometry, coverage of u and q, q>=u and SI bounds are validated in
    P1 with typed violations; the store checks raw values before normalization
    and blocks the result visibly.
