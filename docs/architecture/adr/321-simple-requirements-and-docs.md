# 321: Simple requirements and useful documentation

## Context

Mandatory tag chains and overlapping process instructions obscured the product’s intended behavior. The product goal remains useful, sound flight preparation.

## Decision

Use stable REQ headings and one independently testable EARS obligation per requirement. Keep calculations, data-source constraints and reference cases in focused architecture/testing documents. Keep future capabilities in requirements and distinguish delivery status in one roadmap.

AGENTS.md is the canonical contributor instruction; CLAUDE.md points to it. Remove tracing registries/tooling, trace-only gates and mandatory attestation/release ceremony. Retain functional/security/P1 tests, hooks, signed commits, product PRs and one independent review under ADR-319.

## Consequences

Ordinary links and precise acceptance conditions support discussion without a replacement matrix. Synthetic numerical evidence remains distinct from operational POH validation. Licence, liability and certification status are unchanged.
